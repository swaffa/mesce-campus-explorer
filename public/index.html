WASD or arrows to walk. Hold Shift to run and press Space to jump outside. Press E for the nearby action. Press F at a shop or fruit tree. Press B beside a seat to sit or stand. Press P on a prayer mat, or R to read in the library. Drag to orbit the camera. Scroll to zoom. Use the View button to switch between follow, close, and aerial cameras. On tablets and phones, tap an open path to walk, double-tap to run, drag to look, and pinch to zoom. A quick two-finger tap jumps. Nearby actions appear as a single touch button.
